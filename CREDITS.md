## Audio (public/audio, added 2026-11-23; the music line is in the in-game credits, src/credits/credits.js)

All supplied by the user. Trimmed to loops and re-encoded mono at 40-48 kbps for weak machines.
- music1.mp3 (scene 1): first 9 minutes of the YouTube compilation "Relaxing Mexico - Chillout Mexican Music, Musica" (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KCCHCSrmF4o). License not confirmed: check before wide release.
- music2.mp3 (scene 2, played by the two street musicians): first 8.6 minutes, before any singing, of "Mexican Hacienda 1950s | Traditional Mexican Mariachi Music and Ambience" (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pj7bH7U3GbM). License not confirmed.
- dirt.mp3, stone.mp3, marble.mp3, gallop.mp3, neigh.mp3, crowd.mp3: the user's sound files "Footsteps Walking on Dirt Field", "Footsteps on Stone" (first 10 s), "Male Footsteps on Marble Floor in Hallway", "Group Of Horses Galloping On Dirt", "Horse Neigh", "Spanish Crowd talking". Source and license not recorded.
- People's voices are synthesized in the browser (public/shell/audio.js), no recordings.
